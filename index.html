Why We Built Ranita

It started with a single text message. It looked completely legitimate.

Within days, scammers convinced someone to transfer money to “protect” their account from fraud. By the time the truth became clear, the money was already gone.

But the financial loss wasn’t the worst part.

What followed was the shame. The embarrassment. The relentless self-doubt. “How could I have been so foolish?”

The truth is—they weren’t foolish. They were trusting.

And scammers exploit that trust every single day.

The scale of the problem is staggering. Millions of dollars are stolen each year through fraudulent messages, fake calls, and increasingly sophisticated schemes. But statistics don’t capture the real cost.

Retirees losing their life savings. Grandparents draining their accounts to help a “grandchild in trouble.” Hard-working people watching decades of careful saving disappear in an instant.

These aren’t just financial crimes. They’re life-altering events.

We’ve seen victims fall into depression. Marriages strained by guilt and blame. People who lose confidence in their own judgment—and with it, their sense of independence.

It doesn’t have to be this way.

That’s why we built Ranita.

We wanted something simple: a tool anyone can use to check whether a message or conversation is suspicious—before it’s too late. No complicated setup. No confusing technology. Just paste a message or select a screenshot and get a clear, honest answer.

And we made one promise above all else: your privacy would never be compromised.

Every analysis happens directly on your iPhone using Apple Intelligence. Your messages are never sent to a server. They’re never stored in a database. They’re never seen by anyone but you.

Because the last thing someone worried about a scam needs is another reason not to trust technology.

Key features

QR code (“quishing”) detection

Automatically extracts and checks hidden URLs inside QR codes in screenshots.

Warning signs & next steps

Clear badges showing the scam category, red flags, and next steps.

Smarter reply protection

Warns when replying is unsafe or drafts a polite refusal when disengaging is safe.

Family passphrase

Three secret words, paired in person and changed every 5 minutes, so a caller who sounds like family can prove it’s them.

Regional official helplines

One-tap access to official anti-scam hotlines and 7726 carrier spam reporting (US, UK, AU, CA, NZ, JP).

Common scams guide

Offline reference for parcel scams, unpaid tolls, bank/police impersonation, pig-butchering, QR quishing, voice clones, and job scams.
